## Handover Recipients

The final platform handover requires sign-off from the following stakeholder groups:

### 1. Executive Leadership
- **Role:** Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- **Focus Areas:**
  - Platform overall strategy alignment
  - Business value realization
  - ROI and cost metrics approval

**Contact:** `stakeholder:executive:cto`

### 2. Engineering Team
- **Role:** VP of Engineering / Engineering Manager
- **Focus Areas:**

  - Code quality and standards compliance
  - Development workflow readiness

**Contact:** `stakeholder:engineering:vp`

**Contact:** `stakeholder:security:ciso`

## Sign-Off Process

### Sign-Off Categories
1. **Performance Sign-Off** - Platform meets all performance KPIs
2. **Security Sign-Off** - Security requirements fully addressed
3. **Compliance Sign-Off** - All regulatory requirements met
4. **Functionality Sign-Off** - All features implemented and tested
5. **Operational Sign-Off** - Platform ready for production

### Sign-Off Workflow
1. Create sign-off request in [`SignOffTracker`](frontend/src/components/maturity/SignOffTracker.tsx)
2. Assign to appropriate stakeholder type
3. Stakeholder reviews evidence
4. Stakeholder approves or requests revisions
5. All sign-offs complete = Platform officially handed over
