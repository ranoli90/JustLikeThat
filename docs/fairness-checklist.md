# Fairness Checklist

## Overview
This checklist ensures that the intake process and candidate profile processing adhere to fairness and anti-discrimination principles.

## Intake Questions & Data Collection

### 1. Inclusive Language
- [ ] All questions use neutral, inclusive language
- [ ] No biased or discriminatory phrasing
- [ ] Questions avoid stereotyping
- [ ] Gender-neutral terminology is used

### 2. Non-Discriminatory Data Fields
- [ ] No questions about protected characteristics (race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability)
- [ ] No questions about marital status, parental status, or sexual orientation
- [ ] No questions about political affiliation or union membership

### 3. Equal Opportunity Practices
- [ ] All candidates are asked the same questions
- [ ] No differential treatment based on demographics
- [ ] Consistent scoring and evaluation criteria
- [ ] Transparent decision-making processes

## Data Processing & Validation

### 4. Bias Detection
- [ ] Automated fairness checks are implemented
- [ ] Outliers and potential biases are flagged
- [ ] Bias mitigation techniques are applied
- [ ] Regular audits of the system for biases

### 5. Data Minimization
- [ ] Only necessary information is collected
- [ ] Data retention policies are clear
- [ ] Data is anonymized where possible
- [ ] Secure data storage and transmission

### 6. Standardized Evaluation
- [ ] Evaluation criteria are documented and consistent
- [ ] Multiple evaluators are used for important decisions
- [ ] Calibration sessions for evaluators
- [ ] Regular reviews of evaluation practices

## Derived Profiles & Recommendations

### 7. Fair Scoring
- [ ] Scoring models are transparent and explainable
- [ ] Model decisions are auditable
- [ ] No discriminatory features in scoring
- [ ] Fairness metrics are tracked and reported

### 8. Inclusive Recommendations
- [ ] Recommendations include diverse candidates
- [ ] No systemic bias in recommendations
- [ ] Recommendations are based on merit
- [ ] Regular audits of recommendation algorithms

### 9. Accessibility
- [ ] Intake form is accessible to people with disabilities
- [ ] Alternative formats available
- [ ] Screen reader compatibility
- [ ] Keyboard navigation support

## Transparency & Accountability

### 10. Transparency
- [ ] Clear information about how data is used
- [ ] Explainable AI principles are followed
- [ ] Candidates can see and contest their profile
- [ ] Fairness reports are publicly available

### 11. Accountability
- [ ] Clear lines of responsibility for fairness
- [ ] Regular training on fair hiring practices
- [ ] Complaint and appeal processes
- [ ] External audits by independent bodies

### 12. Continuous Improvement
- [ ] Regular fairness reviews
- [ ] Feedback mechanisms for candidates
- [ ] Updating processes based on feedback
- [ ] Staying current with fair hiring best practices

## Compliance

### 13. Legal Compliance
- [ ] Adherence to anti-discrimination laws
- [ ] Compliance with equal opportunity regulations
- [ ] Data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- [ ] Accessibility laws (WCAG, ADA)

### 14. Ethical Guidelines
- [ ] Compliance with ethical guidelines for AI and hiring
- [ ] Transparency and accountability
- [ ] Privacy and data protection
- [ ] Fairness and non-discrimination

## Documentation

### 15. Fairness Documentation
- [ ] Fairness policies are documented
- [ ] Training materials for recruiters
- [ ] Fairness metrics and reports
- [ ] Auditing and monitoring procedures

### 16. Reporting
- [ ] Regular fairness reports
- [ ] Disparate impact analysis
- [ ] Audit findings and improvements
- [ ] Stakeholder communication

## Incident Response

### 17. Incident Handling
- [ ] Procedures for handling bias incidents
- [ ] Root cause analysis for biases
- [ ] Remediation plans
- [ ] Communication protocols

### 18. Prevention
- [ ] Proactive bias prevention measures
- [ ] Regular monitoring for biases
- [ ] Employee training
- [ ] Incident response plans

## Conclusion
This checklist serves as a comprehensive guide for ensuring fairness throughout the intake process and candidate profile processing. Regular audits and continuous improvement are essential to maintaining fairness and trust in the system.
